About CB: True Citizens Band is short-range RF (26.965–27.405 MHz). This panel is a realistic simulation with accurate channels and audio. For real live signals from actual antennas, use the Real HF / CB tab (WebSDR / KiwiSDR).
Real CB and HF radio signals can be received through public Software-Defined Radio receivers connected to antennas around the world. Tune them yourself in your browser.

Interactive map of hundreds of public KiwiSDR receivers. Many cover the 11-meter / CB band (26–28 MHz). Click a receiver near a location of interest and tune to ~27 MHz.
